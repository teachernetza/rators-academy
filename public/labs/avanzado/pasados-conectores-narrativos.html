3. The Three Past Tenses

Para contar una buena historia, combinamos estos tres tiempos verbales.

Simple Past

(Acciones terminadas. La acción principal)

"I woke up late and ran to the station."

Past Continuous

(was/were + verb-ing. Describe el fondo o acciones interrumpidas)

"I was listening to music when my phone died."

Past Perfect

(had + past participle. El pasado del pasado. Algo que ya había sucedido)

"When I arrived, the train had already left."

4. Advanced: Past Habits

Cuando hablamos de la ciudad en el pasado, a menudo mencionamos rutinas que ya no hacemos.

Used to + verb

Para hábitos o estados que eran ciertos en el pasado, pero ya no lo son.

Aff: I used to live in the bustling city center.
Neg: I didn't use to like crowded places.

Would + verb

Solo para acciones/rutinas repetitivas en el pasado. ¡No se usa para estados (como live, love, be)!

Correct: Every morning, I would buy coffee at the station.
Incorrect: I would live in Paris.
(Must use "used to live")

5. Pronunciation: Regular Verbs (-ed)

El sufijo "-ed" de los verbos regulares tiene tres sonidos distintos. ¡No siempre se pronuncia como se escribe!

/t/

Después de sonidos sin voz (p, k, f, s, ch, sh)

  • Missed (mis-t)
  • Walked (wok-t)
  • Watched (wach-t)
/d/

Después de sonidos con voz (b, g, v, z, m, n, l, r, vocales)

  • Arrived (ar-riv-d)
  • Realized (ri-al-iz-d)
  • Played (play-d)
/id/

Solo después de los sonidos T o D.
¡Añade una sílaba extra!

  • Waited (way-tid)
  • Wanted (won-tid)
  • Needed (nee-did)

6. Conversation: The Museum

Lee la conversación. Presta atención a los verbos en negrita y los conectores resaltados.

Alex 🧑‍🦱

Hey Sam! How was your weekend in the city?

👨🏻‍🦰 Sam

It was crazy. I wanted to visit the new art exhibition downtown.

Alex 🧑‍🦱

Oh, nice! Did you get in?

👨🏻‍🦰 Sam

Unfortunately, no! While I was taking the subway, there was a huge delay. By the time I arrived, the museum had already closed. 📉

Alex 🧑‍🦱

That's terrible! So, what did you do?

👨🏻‍🦰 Sam

I realized that I hadn't eaten anything all day, so I just bought a pizza and went home. 🍕

10. Conversation: The Concert

Mia y Leo hablan sobre un hobby (ir a conciertos). Observa el uso de los tiempos.

Leo 👦🏽

Did you enjoy the rock concert last night? 🎸

👩🏻 Mia

Leo 👦🏽

Oh no. What happened?

👩🏻 Mia

Leo 👦🏽

Did you miss your favorite song?

👩🏻 Mia

Fortunately, no. They played it at the very end.
