Module 1

The Focus Shift 👀

Why do we use the Passive Voice? Because sometimes, the action or the object is more important than the person who did it.

🏃‍♂️

Active Voice

Focus on the DOER (The person)

"Somebody stole my car!"
TARGET
🚗

Passive Voice

Focus on the RECEIVER (The object)

"My car was stolen!"

The Golden Rule

To make a passive sentence, you ALWAYS need the verb To Be and the Past Participle (V3) of the main verb.

Module 2

Present Simple Passive

Used for facts, routines, manufacturing, and materials.
Formula: am / is / are + Past Participle

Materials & Origins

Coffee is grown in Colombia.

El café se cultiva en Colombia.

These shoes are made of leather.

Estos zapatos están hechos de cuero.

General Facts

English is spoken all over the world.

El inglés se habla en todo el mundo.

The rooms are cleaned every day.

Las habitaciones son limpiadas todos los días.

Pasta is originally made in Italy. 🍝

La pasta es hecha originalmente en Italia.

The house was destroyed in tornados season. 🌪️

La casa fué destruida en temporada de tornados.

Module 3

Past Simple Passive

Used for history, discoveries, inventions, and art.
Formula: was / were + Past Participle

Singular (He, She, It, I)

Use WAS

  • 📱 The telephone was invented by Bell.
  • 🎨 The Mona Lisa was painted by Da Vinci.
AND

Plural (You, We, They)

Use WERE

  • 🏛️ The pyramids were built in Egypt.
  • 🔑 My keys were lost yesterday.
