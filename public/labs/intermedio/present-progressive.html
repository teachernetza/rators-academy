3. The Formula

To build sentences, we need three pieces: Subject + To Be + Verb-ing.

I

He / She / It

8. Negatives & Questions

How to say "No" and how to ask what is happening.

Negative (-)

Add NOT after am/is/are.

  • I am not ('m not) working.
  • He is not (isn't) sleeping.
  • They are not (aren't) reading.

Question (?)

Move am/is/are to the FRONT.

  • Am I working?
  • Is he sleeping?
  • Are they reading?
